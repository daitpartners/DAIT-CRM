# Release 1B owner checklist

Things to click through before Companies & Contacts (release 1B) is accepted. Tick each one and note anything that looks wrong.

The live site starts with no sample companies, so the steps below have you create a few of your own. You can set them to Closed afterwards; companies are never deleted.

## Companies

- [ ] In the sidebar, under Modules, click **Companies**. The list opens, empty if nothing has been added yet.
- [ ] Click **Add company**. Enter a name, choose a country and add a website (for example `https://example-one.com`). Save. The company appears in the list with a number such as CO-0001.
- [ ] Click **Add company** again and type the same company name. A "Possible duplicates" note appears under the form, naming the first company, and you can still save.
- [ ] Now also type the same website as the first company. A red note "This looks like CO-… …" appears and **Save** is greyed out. Change the website to something new; the note goes away and you can save.
- [ ] Use the search box and the Status, Country and Industry filters. The list narrows, the filters stay after you refresh the page, and **Clear filters** brings everything back.
- [ ] Tick two companies, choose **Set status…** and pick Inactive. Confirm. Both rows now read Inactive.

## Company profile

- [ ] Click a company name. Its profile opens with the tabs Overview, Contacts, Locations, Leads & Prospects, Activities, AI Intelligence and Research Sources.
- [ ] Overview shows the details you entered; **Edit** lets you change them and save.
- [ ] Leads & Prospects, Activities, AI Intelligence and Research Sources each say which later release they arrive in.

## Contacts on a company

- [ ] On the **Contacts** tab click **Add contact**. Enter a first name, last name and email address, and save. The contact appears and, as the company's first contact, is marked **Primary**.
- [ ] Add a second contact. Type the first contact's email address: a red note says that person already has this email address and **Save** is greyed out. Use a different email and save.
- [ ] Click **Make primary** on the second contact and confirm. The Primary badge moves to them, and the Companies list shows them as the primary contact.

## Locations

- [ ] On the **Locations** tab click **Add location**. Enter a name, choose a location type and a country, and save. The location appears and the tab count goes up by one.
- [ ] Click **Delete** on that location and confirm. It disappears and the count goes back down.

## Contacts page

- [ ] In the sidebar click **Contacts** (under Companies). Every contact you can see is listed, with a link to their company.
- [ ] Type part of a surname, an email address or a company name in the search box. The list narrows to the matching people. Click a company name to open that company's profile.
- [ ] Use the **Contact type** filter, and click the Name, Company and Email column headings to sort.
- [ ] Click **Add contact**. In the **Company** box type the first few letters of a company and pick it from the list. Enter a name and save. The new contact appears in the list under that company.

## Sales representative visibility

- [ ] Go to Settings, Administration, **Users** and add a colleague (or a second account of your own) with the role **Sales Representative**.
- [ ] Sign in as that user. **Companies** and **Contacts** are empty, even though you added companies as the administrator.
- [ ] As the sales representative, add a company. Only that company is listed, and only its contacts show on the Contacts page. Sign back in as yourself: you see every company, including the new one.

## Spanish labels

- [ ] Go to Settings, **My settings**, change the language to Español and save. The sidebar reads **Empresas** and **Contactos**; the Companies list, a company profile (tabs **Contactos**, **Ubicaciones**) and the Contacts page are in Spanish, including buttons such as **Añadir empresa**, **Añadir contacto** and **Hacer principal**. Change the language back to English.
